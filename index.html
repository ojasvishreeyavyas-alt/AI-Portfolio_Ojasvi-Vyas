PROJECT 02 // PROSPECT SOURCING & QUALIFICATION

AI Lead Research & Qualification Automation

Workflow Architecture

A repeatable prospecting workflow concept designed to collect raw business records, enrich company data, apply LLM-based qualification rules, and output prioritized lead sheets with human review checkpoints.

Discovery Sources / Apify → Structured Sheet / DB → LLM ICP Qualification Gate → Human Verification → Prioritized Outreach List

Business Problem

Manual lead sourcing is slow and prone to inconsistent filtering. Sales teams need a structured method to check company fit against explicit inclusion and exclusion criteria.

What the Workflow Does

  • Collects candidate business records from search and directory discovery sources.
  • Evaluates fit using an AI model guided by strict ICP rules and rejection gates.
  • Outputs fit rationale, source evidence, and follow-up priority while retaining human review for final contact verification.
