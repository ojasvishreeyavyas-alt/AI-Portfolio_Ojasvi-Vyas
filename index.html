02 // AUTONOMOUS CONTENT SYSTEMS

AI Content Research Pipeline

Workflow Automation

End-to-end automated workflows that handle trend analysis, high-signal idea selection, structured drafting, and workspace organization—freeing human minds to focus purely on original creative direction.

01. Trend Analysis & Scraping → 02. AI Scoring & Top-3 Idea Selection → 03. Structured Drafting → 04. Google Docs / Sheets Organization

The Bottleneck

Creative teams spend 70% of their bandwidth on repetitive research, tracking trends across tabs, sorting spreadsheets, and formatting initial drafts before real creative work even begins.

Workflow Architecture

  • Automated Trend Analysis: Scrapes and aggregates emerging topic signals into a centralized Google Sheets repository.
  • Intelligent Idea Selection: Evaluates candidate topics against audience relevance and isolates the top three highest-potential ideas per sprint.
  • Drafting & Organization: Generates structured first drafts and automatically organizes files in Google Docs and Drive for human editorial polish.
